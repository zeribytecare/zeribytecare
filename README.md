## Featured Projects

### ZPay

A flexible payment management and collection platform for recurring, installment, partial, and one-time payments.

**Key features**

- Multi-tenant architecture
- Billing accounts and payment schedules
- Penalties, reminders, and overdue tracking
- Collector and customer portals
- Subscription and cloud synchronization
- Android offline-first support
- Reports, dashboards, and analytics

**Tech stack:** React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Socket.io, Capacitor

---

### Human Resource Management System

An integrated HR platform for managing employees and internal operations.

**Key features**

- Employee records
- Attendance with geolocation
- Payroll processing
- Leave and approval workflows
- Recruitment and disciplinary records
- Asset management
- Reports and notifications
- Real-time messaging

**Tech stack:** React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Socket.io, Docker

---

### Fleet and Hauling ERP

A centralized business platform for hauling, equipment, billing, payroll, and operational records.

**Key features**

- Equipment masterlist
- Dump truck trip tickets
- Fuel and maintenance logs
- Payroll and billing reports
- Inventory and general expenses
- Client and equipment owner records
- Roles, approvals, and printable reports

**Tech stack:** React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, Caddy

---

### ZPOS

A restaurant point-of-sale system for dine-in, takeout, and delivery workflows.

**Key features**

- POS order processing
- Kitchen display workflow
- Menu and inventory management
- Branch support
- Real-time order updates
- Sales analytics and reports
- Payment breakdown and peak-hour insights

**Tech stack:** React, Zustand, Node.js, Sequelize, PostgreSQL, Redis, Socket.io
